Delete your CartFriend account

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open CartFriend and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Update Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

No longer have the app?

Email from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: username, email address, display name and password.
  • Your sign-in sessions and the invitation codes you created.
  • Your family membership. Items you were buying become unassigned.
  • If you were the only member of your family, the family and all its lists and items are deleted too.

What stays

  • If other people are in your family, the family and its shared lists stay for them. Items are not linked to you after deletion. If you were the only admin, another member becomes admin.
  • Encrypted backups may contain your data for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
