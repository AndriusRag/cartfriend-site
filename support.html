Common questions

How do I invite my family?
Family admins open Menu → Family Management → Generate new invitation code and share the 6-digit code. It is valid for 12 hours.

How do I take an item to buy?
Open the item and choose yourself under "Who is buying this?". Others will see it as reserved.

How do I change my name?
Menu → Update Account.
